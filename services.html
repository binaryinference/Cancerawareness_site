Our services

Supporting the cancer journey

Our work is centred around practical and emotional support for cancer patients, their families and their caregivers.

Patient transport

We assist cancer patients with transportation to and from medical appointments, including:

  • Doctor consultations
  • Private hospital appointments
  • Chemotherapy appointments
  • Other appropriate healthcare-related appointments

Where appropriate, patients are transported to their appointment and assisted with their return journey home.

Home-based psychosocial support

We provide psychosocial support through appointments conducted in patients' homes. Support may include:

  • Individual patient sessions
  • Family sessions
  • Patient-and-family sessions
  • Emotional support
  • Support during the cancer journey

Our aim is to provide patients and families with a safe and supportive environment in which they can discuss the challenges they are experiencing.

Family support

Cancer affects the entire family.

We provide support to family members and caregivers and, where appropriate, facilitate sessions involving both the patient and their family.

Patient navigation & care coordination

We assist patients in navigating aspects of their cancer-care journey by helping coordinate support and facilitating appropriate referrals and follow-up.

Our role is to help patients and families better understand and navigate the different services involved in their care.

We do not replace doctors, hospitals or other registered healthcare professionals.

Who we support

Cancer patients and their families

Our services are focused on supporting:

  • Cancer patients
  • Families of cancer patients
  • Caregivers
  • Communities requiring cancer awareness and education

Our support model is community-based, allowing us to provide assistance within patients' communities and homes where appropriate.

Our impact

What we aim to achieve

Our work is intended to contribute to:

Better access to healthcare

Helping patients reach medical appointments and chemotherapy.

Greater psychosocial support

Providing emotional and psychosocial support to patients and families.

Stronger family support

Helping families and caregivers navigate the challenges associated with cancer.

Improved patient navigation

Helping patients and families better coordinate and navigate available services.

Greater cancer awareness

Bringing cancer education and awareness into communities.

Long-term community capacity

Building towards a dedicated cancer support and palliative-care centre.

Please note: Sibusisiwe Cancer Care Center does not provide medical diagnosis or replace professional medical treatment. Patients should continue to consult their doctors and healthcare providers for medical advice and treatment.
