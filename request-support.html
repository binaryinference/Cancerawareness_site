Referrals / request support

Need support?

If you or someone you know is affected by cancer and requires support, you can contact Sibusisiwe Cancer Care Center to enquire about available assistance.

Our support may include

  • Patient transport
  • Psychosocial support
  • Family support
  • Patient navigation
  • Referral and care coordination

How to request support

  1. Contact us

    Use the form, call or message us.

  2. Tell us what you need

    Tell us about the type of support required.

  3. We assess your request

    We will determine what assistance or referral may be appropriate.

  4. We coordinate where needed

    Where necessary, we coordinate with the relevant healthcare professional or service provider.

Important: Sibusisiwe Cancer Care Center does not provide medical diagnosis or replace professional medical treatment. Patients should continue to consult their doctors and healthcare providers for medical advice and treatment.
